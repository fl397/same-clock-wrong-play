# Limitations

1. **Prior discovery is credited, not ours.** The scorer-latency phenomenon, the shot-based offset calibration, the held-out-turnover validation idea and the spacing/eFG example were first shown on 10 games by the ismayc/tracking-study repository (commit `d21f8e3c`). This work is the season-scale validation, the audit of downstream consequences, and the resource layer; it is not a new alignment algorithm.
2. **Historical corpus only.** Findings apply to the public 2015-16 SportVU/PBP pairing studied here. They are not evidence of equivalent offsets in Second Spectrum, Hawk-Eye, other NBA seasons, or other sports. "The four-second rule" is shorthand for this corpus, nothing more.
3. **A fixed +4 s shift equals or beats game-specific correction in this corpus** (held-out turnover agreement 0.942 vs 0.932; no class in which per-game calibration wins). The per-game layer is therefore a quality/uncertainty resource, not an improved correction.
4. **About 30 % of games carry lower-confidence metadata**: MULTIMODAL (19.0 %) and DRIFTING (10.6 %) agreement curves; DRIFTING games retain ≈ 7 % wrong-team turnover windows after the shift. Offsets are identified only to a 2–3 s plateau in most games; the argmax lead sits a median ≈ 1 s above the rising edge of the curve, so per-game leads are upper-biased estimates of the pure clock offset.
5. **Validation labels are themselves heuristic.** Team-in-possession labels come from a nearest-player-to-ball rule; agreement rates measure consistency between that rule and the play-by-play team, not frame-accurate ground truth (none exists for this corpus).
6. **Consequence metrics were measured on existing detectors and analyses** (on-ball screen detections, time-window analyses, one transition-offense analysis) as they were, not on re-tuned versions.
7. **Live-play classification is rule-based** (`configs/LIVE_PLAY_CLASSIFIER.yaml`, frozen before results): semantic PBP/clock rules with movement as corroboration only; 5.3 % of segments are AMBIGUOUS.
8. **Data rights (open).** Raw and corrected tracking data and all frame-/event-/possession-level derived tables are withheld pending a redistribution basis; the repository ships code, configuration, per-game scalar metadata and aggregates. The stages that need withheld intermediates are marked in `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.
9. **The eFG/spacing table's corrected axis is the per-game lead** (the artifact as produced); the abstract says "once the clocks are aligned" without specifying which correction. The fixed +4 s shift performed at least as well on held-out turnovers; the table was not separately regenerated under +4 s.
