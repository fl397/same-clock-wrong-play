# Data availability

Availability and redistribution rights are different things. The inputs to this study are publicly *accessible*, but we have not established a rights basis for *redistributing* them, and neither hosting on GitHub nor an MIT license on our code establishes one. Until that question is settled the repository follows the restricted form below.

**The software in this repository is released under the MIT License. Third-party NBA tracking and play-by-play data are not covered by this license and are not redistributed here.**

## Not redistributed

- Raw 2015-16 SportVU tracking files (the public `NBA-Player-Movements` mirror) — source pointer only.
- stats.nba.com PlayByPlayV3 rows — source pointer only; acquisition method and column list in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.
- Any frame-level, event-level, possession-level or player-level derived table: de-duplicated frames, possession intervals, live-play segment tables, screen detections, per-shot spacing tables, and the corrected (re-timed) corpus itself.

## Included

- **Code and configuration.** Every producer (`src/`), the frozen rule files (`configs/`) and the reproduction script (`scripts/run_all.sh`), under the MIT License.
- **Per-game scalar alignment metadata.** `results/clock_alignment_v1.csv` (with `results/clock_alignment_v1_manifest.json`) and `results/taxonomy_by_game.csv`: one row per game holding the calibrated lead, the quality class, shot- and turnover-agreement counts and rates, plateau width, half-game leads and drift flags; `results/global_shift_by_game.csv` holds per-game held-out agreement at 0 s, +4 s and the per-game lead. These tables contain no coordinates, no timestamps of individual events and no player-level information — they are per-game scalars derived from the public sources, not redistributed tracking data. If the rights question is resolved against publishing even this, they can be replaced by their season-level aggregates without affecting any figure.
- **Aggregate results.** Validation, consequence and prevalence statistics (`results/*.json`, season-level CSV summaries) and the figure-ready aggregates behind the two figures — game- or corpus-level summaries only.
- **Reconstruction instructions.** Every reported number can be regenerated by a user who obtains the public sources independently (`docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`); a 25-game check from a fresh clone reproduced the shipped per-game leads, statuses and agreement rates exactly (`docs/FRESH_CLONE_TEST.md`).

## If a redistribution basis is established later

The repository would then state which data are redistributed and on what basis, and would add the withheld intermediate tables needed to run the screen-consequence and two-by-two stages without rebuilding them. No such statement is made now.
